Har Mushkil Ka Hal,Qur'an o Sunnah Ki Roshni Mein

Dar ul Rehmat mein hum Qur'ani aayat aur Sunnat-e-Nabwi (ﷺ) ki roshni mein aapki zindagi ke rohani masail, pareshaniyon aur bimariyon ka shar'i ilaj karte hain. Umeed ki ek nayi kiran ke sath, shifa ki taraf apna pehla qadam aaj hi uthaiye.

Hafiz Amir Shahzad Saifi

Founder & Rohani Mushir

Alhamdulillah, mujhe deeni aur rohani rehnumai ke maidan mein 11 saal se zyada ka tajurba hasil hai. Is arsay ke dauran main Qur'an-o-Sunnah ki roshni mein aur shar'i usoolon ke mutabiq deeni aur rohani rehnumai faraham karta aa raha hoon. Har shakhs ki baat ko tawajjoh, ikhlaas aur mukammal raazdari ke sath sunta hoon aur Allah Ta'ala se us ke liye behtareen rehnumai, asani aur khair ki dua karta hoon. Mera maqsad logon ko Allah Ta'ala ki taraf ruju, du'a, sabr aur sahih deeni rehnumai ki taraf rehnumai dena hai. Mera yaqeen hai ke asal shifa, rehmat aur kamyabi sirf Allah Ta'ala ke ikhtiyar mein hai.
